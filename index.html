📖 Quick Guide

How to use the app

• Add Expense — Click "Add Expense" in the sidebar, fill in the amount, category, date and optional description, then click Save.

• Other Category — Select "Other" from the category dropdown to create a custom category name for any expense.

• Reports — Filter your transactions by date range and category. Delete any transaction using the trash icon.

• Analytics — Visual breakdown of spending by category with pie chart and progress bars.

• Settings — Update your profile, change password, and configure preferences like currency and notifications.
