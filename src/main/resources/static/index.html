📖 Quick Guide

How to use the app

• Dashboard — See your balance and total expenses at a glance. Use the "Add Balance" field to top up your balance.

• Add Expense — Record any expense. Choose "Other (Custom)" to create your own category.

• Reports — Filter transactions by date range and category. Delete any transaction using 🗑.

• Analytics — Pie chart and progress bar breakdown of your spending.

• Settings — Update profile, change password, choose currency, export data.
